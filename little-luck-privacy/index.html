🍀 Little Luck

Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Effective Date: August 26, 2026
Developer / Organization: K2 Labs (Min Kaung Khant)
Package Name: com.k2labs.littleluck

1. Overview

Little Luck is a gentle, local-first gratitude and lucky moments journaling application. Your personal reflections, thoughts, and moments belong solely to you. We do not track, profile, or sell your personal data.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

  • Local Storage (Default): All notes, categories, and streak counters are stored strictly on your local device.
  • Optional Cloud Sync (Google Sign-In & Firestore): If you choose to enable Cloud Sync, your Google email address, Firebase User ID (UID), and synchronized moments are securely stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted between the Little Luck application and cloud servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS.

3. Account & Data Deletion Instructions

How to Request Permanent Deletion of Your Account and Data

Users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated cloud sync data at any time without restriction:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual moment directly in the app via the three-dots menu on any note card.
  • Full Account & Cloud Data Deletion: Send an email request to minkaungkhant@gmail.com with the subject Little Luck - Data Deletion Request and provide your associated Google email address.
  • Processing Time: Upon receiving your request, all user identifiers, backups, and database records stored in Google Cloud Firestore will be permanently and irreversibly purged within 7 business days.

4. Third-Party Services

Little Luck integrates with Google Play Services and Firebase Authentication / Firestore solely for optional user authentication and encrypted data backup.

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out directly at minkaungkhant@gmail.com.
