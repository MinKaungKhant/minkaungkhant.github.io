Privacy Policy

S8Pro Smart Ring Connector

Developer: K2 Labs  |  Package: com.k2labs.s8proconnector

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Privacy summary: S8Pro Smart Ring Connector is designed to work locally with compatible S8Pro Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices. The current release does not require an account, does not contain advertising, does not use the Android Advertising ID, and does not transmit user data to K2 Labs servers.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how S8Pro Smart Ring Connector (the “App”), provided by K2 Labs, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

2. Information the App accesses

The App accesses only the information and device capabilities needed to provide its core Bluetooth-device and reminder features.

  • Nearby Bluetooth device information: The App scans for and connects to compatible S8Pro BLE devices. During this process, Android may provide nearby-device information such as Bluetooth identifiers, device names, advertised services, and connection state.
  • S8Pro device data: While connected, the App may receive device-generated information needed for supported features, such as counter values, device modes or profiles, battery information, connection state, and supported device settings.
  • Task and reminder information: Information you enter or configure for counting tasks and reminders may be stored locally on your Android device so the App can provide those features across sessions.
  • Notifications: With your permission, the App may display local notifications for active tasks, connection-related functionality, and reminders. The App does not read the content of your notifications from other apps.

3. Bluetooth and location permissions

On Android 12 and later, the App uses the Android Nearby Devices Bluetooth permissions (BLUETOOTH_SCAN and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) to discover and communicate with compatible S8Pro devices. Bluetooth scanning is declared as not being used to derive your physical location.

On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires location permission for certain BLE scanning operations. The App may request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION only on those older Android versions for Bluetooth discovery compatibility. The App does not use that permission to track, store, or transmit your physical location.

4. Data collection and transmission

The current release of the App does not transmit personal information, S8Pro device data, task data, or usage data to K2 Labs servers. The App does not require an internet connection for its core functionality and does not include a K2 Labs backend service for collecting App data.

The current release also does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services that collect user data on behalf of K2 Labs.

5. Data sharing

K2 Labs does not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the App because the current release does not transmit such information to K2 Labs or third-party services.

6. Advertising

The App does not display third-party advertisements and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

7. Accounts

The App does not require or provide user account creation or sign-in in the current release.

8. Local storage, retention, and deletion

App settings, task information, reminder configuration, and other supported App state may be stored locally on your Android device. Local data remains on the device until it is removed through an available App feature, Android app-data controls, or by uninstalling the App.

Because the current release does not upload App data to K2 Labs servers, K2 Labs does not maintain a server-side copy of this local App data.

9. Security

The App relies on Android platform security, application sandboxing, runtime permission controls, and Bluetooth security mechanisms provided by Android and the connected device. No method of electronic storage or device communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the App is designed to minimize data exposure by processing its supported data locally.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. K2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current release of the App.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s features, data practices, permissions, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.
