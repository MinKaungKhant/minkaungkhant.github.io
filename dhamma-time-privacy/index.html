Privacy Policy

Dhamma Time

Developer: K2 Labs  |  Package: com.k2labs.dhammatime

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Privacy summary: Dhamma Time is designed as a simple meditation timer that works locally on your Android device. The current release does not require an account, does not contain advertising, does not use the Android Advertising ID, and does not transmit personal data or app usage data to K2 Labs servers.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dhamma Time (the “App”), provided by K2 Labs, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

2. Information the App accesses and stores

The App accesses only the device capabilities and local information needed to provide its meditation timer features.

  • Timer and session state: The App keeps the information needed to run an active meditation timer, including the selected duration and timer state.
  • App settings: Preferences such as default duration, seconds display, bell behavior, screen behavior, theme, and language may be stored locally on your device using Android application storage.
  • Notifications: With your permission, the App displays local notifications so an active meditation timer can continue to operate and inform you when a configured interval is reached.
  • Vibration: If enabled by you, the App may use device vibration as part of meditation timer alerts.
  • Screen and background operation: The App may keep the device awake or run a foreground service while a meditation timer is active so the timer can continue reliably when the App is not visible.

3. Notifications and timer sounds

Dhamma Time may request notification permission on supported Android versions. Notifications are used only for meditation timer functionality, such as showing an active timer and informing you when a timer interval is reached.

Timer bell or meditation sounds used by the current release are processed locally on the device. The App does not record audio from your microphone.

4. Data collection and transmission

The current release of Dhamma Time does not transmit personal information, meditation timer information, settings, or usage data to K2 Labs servers.

The current release does not require an internet connection for its core meditation timer functionality and does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services that collect user data on behalf of K2 Labs.

5. Data sharing

K2 Labs does not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through Dhamma Time because the current release does not transmit such information to K2 Labs or third-party services.

6. Advertising

Dhamma Time does not display third-party advertisements and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

7. Accounts

Dhamma Time does not require or provide user account creation or sign-in in the current release.

8. Local storage, retention, and deletion

App preferences and supported timer state may be stored locally on your Android device. Local data remains on the device until it is changed or removed through the App, cleared through Android app-data controls, or removed by uninstalling the App.

Because the current release does not upload App data to K2 Labs servers, K2 Labs does not maintain a server-side copy of this local App data.

9. Security

Dhamma Time relies on Android platform security, application sandboxing, and runtime permission controls. The App is designed to minimize data exposure by keeping its supported timer data and preferences on the device.

10. Children’s privacy

Dhamma Time does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The current release does not require an account and does not transmit personal data to K2 Labs servers.

11. Future Dhamma content features

Dhamma Time may add optional Dhamma listening or other online content features in future releases. If those features change the App’s permissions, network access, third-party services, or data practices, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant release.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Dhamma Time’s features, permissions, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.
